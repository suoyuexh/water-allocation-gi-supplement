# Supplemental materials

**A Bi-Level Multiagent Model of Water Allocation Policies and Urban Green Infrastructure Investment**  

[Download the supplemental materials (PDF)](supplemental_material_asce.pdf)

The supplement documents the water trading, withdrawal quota, and progressive tariff modules and their implementation in the bi-level multiagent model. It includes the city-scale model equations and notation, the decentralized and centralized benchmark formulations, the solution algorithms, the lower Colorado River case-study setup, the model calibration and validation, and additional sensitivity results for hydroclimatic and green infrastructure implementation scenarios.

The manuscript was prepared for the *Journal of Water Resources Planning and Management*. Its references to Sections S1–S7, Tables S1–S14, and Figs. S1–S6 point to this PDF.
